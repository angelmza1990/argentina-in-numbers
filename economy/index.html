The minimum wage and the minimum pension

Converted across the four currency changes since 1965 (13 zeros) and adjusted by the CPI. The collapse of 1989–90 is real: the minimum wage stayed at 20,000 australes for over a year while prices rose about fortyfold. The poverty line is for one adult; a family of four needs about 3.1 times as much. 2007–2015 is shaded: the official CPI understated inflation then, which inflates real values in those years.
