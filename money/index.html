Argentina in Numbers

Money & rates

The Central Bank's dollars, what the world charges Argentina to borrow, whether a peso deposit beats inflation, and how much money and credit the economy has.

Central Bank reserves

Gross international reserves, US$ billions, end of each month. Gross reserves include swaps with other central banks and banks' dollar deposits held at the Central Bank, so net reserves are lower.

Country risk

The extra yield, in basis points (100 bp = 1 percentage point), that Argentine dollar bonds pay over US Treasuries (JP Morgan EMBI). Logarithmic scale.

Does a peso deposit beat inflation?

The annual rate on 30-day term deposits against inflation over the following twelve months, and the real return: what a month in a term deposit gained or lost after inflation, annualized.
