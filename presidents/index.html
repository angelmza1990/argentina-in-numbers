Argentina in Numbers

By presidency

The economy under each president since the return of democracy in 1983, measured the same way for everyone: from the month before taking office to the last full month in office. Numbers describe what happened during a term, not what caused it; many outcomes reflect decisions, shocks and world prices from before or outside each government.

Every term

Interim presidents of December 2001 (Ramón Puerta, Adolfo Rodríguez Saá, Eduardo Camaño) are left out. Series start at different dates: the dollar since 1991 (fixed at one peso until 2001), reserves since 1996, country risk since 1999, unemployment since 2003; poverty uses the half-year published closest to the start and end. Inflation in 2007–2015 is the official figure, widely considered understated.
